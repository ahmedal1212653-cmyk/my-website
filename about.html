About Fortnite

Fortnite is a cultural phenomenon that blends shooting, building, and creative play into one fast-paced battle royale experience.

From constant updates and live events to unique crossovers with famous franchises, Fortnite keeps players engaged with fresh content and evolving gameplay.

GameOn brings you the latest Fortnite news, guides, and community highlights so you can stay ahead in every match.

Live Events

Special in-game events bring the Fortnite world to life with concerts, story moments, and seasonal challenges.

Creative Mode

Build your own islands, play custom mini-games, and share creations with the Fortnite community.

Cross-Platform Play

Play on PC, consoles, and mobile devices with the same account and progress across platforms.
